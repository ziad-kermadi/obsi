Your mind is like an overly helpful friend.

Noticing and naming 

Dropping anchor

How can we tell we’re hooked:
- Our thoughts are commands we must obey or rules we must follow
- Our thoughts are very important - we must give them full attention
- Our thoughts are the truth - we must believe them

Unhooking techniques:
- Naming the process
- Helpfulness vs Truth
	- heard it before ? Do i gain anything by listening to it again ?
	- What do I get for buying into this thought 
- Thanking your mind for trying to be helpful
- The silly voice technique

We are not trying to get rid of the thoughts but just to unhook from them

Subtitles (funny ?) for painful images

Thinking self vs Noticing self

Value goals instead of outcome goals

If youre in shit and have a companion, which one would you want it to be ? -> self-compassion
- Acknowledge your pain honestly
- If someone you deeply care about is suffering how would you treat them ?
